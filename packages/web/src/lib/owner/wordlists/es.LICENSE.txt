es.txt — the Spanish passphrase wordlist (encryption spec §6.3, brief §15.2)

es.txt is a derived work: 7,776 words selected from a frequency list, filtered
with a dictionary and two treebanks, and reviewed by a fluent reader. It is
built by packages/web/scripts/build-wordlist-es.mjs from the inputs below,
which are not in this repository; es.sources.json pins each one by SHA-256 and
the build refuses an input whose hash differs. The words are in the list; the
frequency counts, lemmas, tags and glosses of the sources are not.

Because the frequency list and the dictionary are share-alike, es.txt itself
is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
That licence covers this one file; nothing else in the repository is under it.
